Built for stablecoin settlement

Arc separates consensus from execution. You get sub second finality, USDC denominated fees, and a single canonical USDC without wrappers.

Deterministic finality

Malachite BFT finalizes every block in under one second. One confirmation on Arc carries the same guarantee as dozens on other chains.

  • Single confirmation for bridges
  • No reorg handling needed
  • Immediate offchain effects

USDC is the gas token

CCTP burn and mint

USDC never locks. It burns on source and mints natively on destination. No wrapped USDC, no liquidity pools.

Move funds in three steps

01Approve and burn

Approve TokenMessenger V2 and call depositForBurn. With Forwarding Service the burn includes the Circle fee so the recipient receives the full amount.

depositForBurnWithHook(amount, domain, recipient, usdc, 0x00, maxFee, 1000, hook)
02Attestation by Circle

Circle Iris signs the message. Poll the attestation API or let the Forwarding Service submit the mint for you.

Iris API and Forwarding Service, about 15 min standard, under 3s via Relay for EVM routes
03Mint on destination

Call receiveMessage on the destination MessageTransmitter V2. Gas is paid on destination unless you used forwarding.

One confirmation on Arc is final. Check ArcScan for the mint.
